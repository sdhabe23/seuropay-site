Why pay merchant fees on card transfer if a better infrastructure already exists in EU?

With a potential to turn every smartphone into a point of sale terminal and every business into a digital powerhouse. We aren't just building a payment app, but a payment ecosystem that works for Europe (initially focused on Estonia) and adds money back to European economy.

Every minute, a merchant waits for their money, and every cent they pay to a card network is a drag on the economy. SeuroPay removes that drag. We are building a system that has the potential to boost the economy.

  • Massive Margin Recovery
  • Instant Cashflow
  • Zero Chargeback Fraud
  • Better Conversion & Security
  • No Interchange Leakage
  • No Card Network Tax

Scan, Authenticate, Done

A seamless mobile experience that connects directly to your existing bank account. No card numbers to type, no plastic to carry, and total control over your financial data.

  • Every transaction is protected by Face ID or Touch ID or PIN ensuring that only you can authorize a payment.
  • Recipient's name confirmation to validate every transaction.
  • Connect SeuroPay directly to your existing bank account for real-time transfers without the need to "top up" a digital wallet.
  • Effortlessly scan/ tap and pay Euros at a store or send them to your family or friends.
  • Enjoy a seamless payment experience with no hidden fees or charges.

Instant Liquidity & Analytics

Track every transaction as it happens. Access automated bookkeeping, manage refunds instantly, and view deep customer insights through a professional, real-time dashboard.

  • A professional dashboard of your daily transactions and cashflow.
  • Use the portal for handling your refunds and chargebacks.
  • Deep customer insights to help you make informed business decisions.
  • Simplify your bookkeeping with automated reports and analytics.

Zero Hardware Onboarding

Get verified as a verified merchant and start accepting payments with your mobile device. No expensive card terminals or monthly rental fees, but just a simple, secure QR-based/ NFC based system designed for the speed and profitability of local business.

  • Leverage a simple, secure system that uses QR codes and NFC technology for rapid customer checkout.
  • Eliminate overhead costs by removing the need for expensive physical card terminals or high monthly rental fees.
  • Maximize local business margins with a payment system built specifically for the speed and profitability requirements of modern retail.
  • Turn your mobile device into a point-of-sale (POS) system, allowing you to accept payments anywhere, anytime.

Developer First Integration

Clean, robust APIs and SDKs built for bigger merchants. Enable high conversion Account-to-Account (A2A) payments on your website or self service point of sale while maintaining full brand control and reducing transaction failure rates.

  • Enable seamless Account-to-Account (A2A) transfers that bypass traditional card networks to boost checkout success.
  • Deploy SeuroPay's payment technology across your website or at physical self-service points of sale.
  • Maintain complete control over your brand’s look and feel throughout the entire payment journey.
